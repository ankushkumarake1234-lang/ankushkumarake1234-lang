## 📌 Featured Projects & Work Context

### 1. 🗣️ PALASH — AI-Powered Multilingual Education Technology
*Associated with Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Work*

- **Problem Statement:** Teachers delivering Mother Tongue-Based Multilingual Education (MTB-MLE) in tribal regions face significant resource gaps due to a lack of localized teaching materials in languages such as **Ho**, **Mundari**, and **Santhali**.
- **Architecture & Exploration:** Designed an AI-assisted translation and curriculum generation workflow covering Hindi-to-mother-tongue translation, FLN (Foundational Literacy and Numeracy) lesson scripts, contextual assessment prompts, synthesized audio generation, and voice-to-voice translation.
- **Technologies:** React, Node.js, Python, AI/NLP APIs, Audio Synthesis Models.
- **Status:** 🧪 Active Research & Project Exploration
